# Historically plausible keys and negative checks

The investigation began as a genuine search for B1's key. These negative
results explain why the project returned to the Declaration; they are not proof
that every conceivable transformation of each document fails.

| Candidate family | Frozen result | Scope note |
|---|---:|---|
| [Virginia Declaration of Rights](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/virginia-declaration-of-rights), Article XVI | adjusted `p = 0.3779` | Direct book indexing is impossible at 68 words; two preregistered cyclic extensions were negative. |
| [Full 1776 Virginia Declaration of Rights](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/virginia-declaration-of-rights) | adjusted `p = 0.3621` | Four direct-character/cyclic-initial candidates; independent exact reproduction. |

The exact [John K. Read 1791 *New Ahiman Rezon* catalog witness](https://catalog.huntington.org/record=b1686848)
and Hutchinson 1800 American
edition were identified but were not openly available as exact scans/OCR. The
Hutchinson imprint is New York: Isaac Collins, for Alexandria booksellers
Cottom & Stewart—not an Alexandria printing. [The Library of Congress catalog
record is LCCN 36031339](https://lccn.loc.gov/36031339). Negative neighboring editions do
not reject those exact editions.

The alleged [“1720 Chatelain map” plaintext](https://www.reddit.com/r/UnresolvedMysteries/comments/379gri/comment/njgk229/)
was tested separately as a claim,
not treated as a plausible solve. In the first 520 letters, it violates 208 of
B1's 222 repeated-position equalities across 115 cipher numbers, so no fixed
direct book-key mapping can produce it. The claimant supplied no deterministic
map traversal. This disproves that posted plaintext, not every future,
independently specified spatial transform of the map.

Only the compact core needed for the final B1/B2 result is packaged here. The
full exploratory workspace included exact scans, OCR, preregistrations,
registrations, null ledgers, and independent verifiers; several scan corpora
were too large or legally unsuitable for redistribution. The reported negative
results are therefore contextual evidence, not part of the repository's core
reproduction claim.
